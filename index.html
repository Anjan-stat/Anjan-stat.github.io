Research focus

I work on Bayesian methods for directional and compositional data — building samplers and spatial models that hold up on real imaging and genomics data, not just in simulation.

My PhD research developed Bayesian models for fiber-direction estimation in diffusion MRI, including a spatial prior for angular continuity (SPACE) and a generalized multi-fiber extension of the Ball-and-Stick model. A separate thread of that work — sampling efficiently on the simplex by embedding it into a circular manifold — became the CircSimplex R package below.

05

Projects

CircSimplex

R package · MCMC · directional statistics

An R package implementing circular-embedding proposal steps for simplex-valued vectors: it embeds a pair of compositional components into a circle, perturbs the angle with a von Mises proposal, and maps the result back onto the simplex. Built as a Metropolis-Hastings proposal mechanism for compositional / proportion data — the computational core of the simplex-sampling research above.

source →

SPACE — Spatial Prior for Angular Continuity Estimation

Bayesian modeling · dMRI · neuroimaging

A Bayesian model incorporating spatial neighborhood information into fiber-direction estimation from diffusion MRI, applied to whole-brain structural data for automatic model selection and resolving fiber-crossing.

Generalized Ball-and-Stick Model

Statistical computing · multi-fiber estimation

A computationally efficient, generalized multi-fiber extension of the standard Ball-and-Stick Model for dMRI, removing its single-fiber limitation.
